# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience
for everybody, whatever their background, and to keep the discussion about
the mathematics.

## What is expected

* Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help.
* Be precise. Most disagreements here are about a definition, and a
  definition is usually written down somewhere.
* Give credit. If a piece of the engine came from a paper, a spike or a
  pull request, say so.

## What is not accepted

* Personal attacks, harassment of any kind, and insults.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing somebody's private information without their permission.
* Deliberately misleading claims about what SAMOSA checks. If the engine
  cannot check something, the honest answer is "it cannot check that", and
  the report says so.

## Reporting

Open an issue on the repository, or write to the maintainer. Reports are
handled in the order they arrive, and the reporter is not asked to
apologise for reporting.

## Scope

This project is about mathematics, so the standard is a mathematical one:
a claim that does not hold is a claim that does not hold, whoever made it.
